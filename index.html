GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
to know.

What size of funding can I enquire about?

CapitalWorks focuses on enquiries above ₹2 crore. Lenders assess each business and funding need.

Does CapitalWorks provide the loan directly?

CapitalWorks facilitates introductions; the lender assesses, decides and sets terms.

What types of businesses are eligible?

Lenders assess business history, revenue, cash flows, obligations and documents.

How quickly can funding be arranged?

Timing depends on documents and lender review. Approval and disbursal dates are not guaranteed.

Are the loan terms and rates fixed on this website?

No. The lender sets rates, security, fees, terms and repayment schedule.
