What is the initial eligibility benchmark?

We use annual revenue above ₹2 crore as an initial screening benchmark. Investor criteria also depend on sector, growth stage, business performance and the proposed raise.

Which industries do you support?

We support businesses across manufacturing, healthcare, logistics, retail and consumer, technology, education, food and hospitality, and import-export.

Does CapitalWorks invest in my company?

CapitalWorks supports fundraising preparation and potential investor introductions. Investment decisions are made by the company and investors.

Is an equity raise or investment guaranteed?

No. Investor interest, due diligence, valuation, terms and closing are not guaranteed. Any investment is subject to investor review and agreement between the parties.

Does equity funding affect company ownership?

Equity investment generally involves issuing or transferring an ownership interest. The company and investor discuss and agree on the structure and terms.
