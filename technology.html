What size of technology business funding can I discuss?

CapitalWorks focuses on business loans and projects above ₹2 crore. Final amount and eligibility are assessed by the lender.

Can funding support equipment or business expansion?

Equipment finance and business term loans may be potential routes. Product fit depends on lender policy, project details and assessment.

Does CapitalWorks approve or disburse the loan?

CapitalWorks helps coordinate lender matching and applications. The financial institution independently assesses, approves, sets terms and disburses any facility.

Are loan rates and collateral requirements fixed?

No. Pricing, security, repayment terms and conditions vary by lender and borrower profile.
